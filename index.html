Introduction & Guide

ABOUT OXYGEN ART & CRAFT

Everything you need to know about Oxygen art & craft, its origin, collections, login, buying, and selling.

1. What Oxygen art & craft Does & Where It Is Made

Oxygen art & craft is an art & craft platform created in India (West Bengal) to showcase handcrafted artworks and connect creators with collectors.

2. Artworks & Categories Available

Available categories include Mandala Art, Lippan Art, Charcoal, Water Color, Mehndi Art, and Alpona—ranging from detailed paper drawings to textured wall crafts.

3. How Login / Artist ID Works

Click the User icon (top right) to open Login, sign up / create account, view your user ID, reset password, or return to store.

4. How to Buy & How to Sell

To Buy: Go to BUY, filter by price/artist/medium/size, add drawings to your bag or click Buy, enter your details, and send via WhatsApp.
To Sell: Open SELL, fill out artwork details, check the agreement box, add your details & notes, and submit.
